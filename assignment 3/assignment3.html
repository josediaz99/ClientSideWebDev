how to enter your first powerlifting meet

pick a federation and purchase a membership

  • usapl

    leading drug tested powerlifting organization in the united states of America

  • powerlifting america

    the national governing body for the sport of powerlifting in the United States of America

  • uspa

    created by lifters with the goal of ensuring lifters are provided an oportunity to showcasing their efforts

find a meet near you you can compete in

make sure the meet is hosted by your federation!!!!

  1. local

    requirements

    • membership
    • equipment meeting standards (not always enforced)

  2. state

    requirements

    • membership
    • equitment meeting standards
    • some states require a qualifying total or meet

  3. regional

    requirements

    • up to meet director

  4. national

    requirements

    • membership
    • equipment meeting standards
    • qualifying total
    • federation approved logos
