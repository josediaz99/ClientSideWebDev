Welcome to the Irron Furries

We are a Chicago based barbell club that focuses on strength training and powerlifting.

Our mission is to provide a supportive and inclusive environment for all lifters, regardless of their experience level.

Our Team

We have a diverse team of experienced coaches, athletes, photographers and more who are passionate about helping others succeed.

Join us for training sessions, competitions, and community events!

Join Us

We offer a variety of programs and resources to help you reach your fitness goals.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced lifter, we have something for everyone.
